# Chrome Web Store listing – Remote Radar

**Name:** Remote Radar – remote job filter for LinkedIn
**Category:** Productivity
**Language:** English
**Summary (max 132 chars):** See if a "remote" LinkedIn job is really open to you: worldwide, your region, or locked to one country.

## Description
LinkedIn's "Remote" filter can't tell "work from anywhere" from "remote, but US residents only". Remote Radar reads each posting and puts a badge on every job card, so you can see at a glance whether you can actually take it.

🌍 Worldwide · ✅ Fits your country/region · 🔒 Locked to another country · 🧭 Region or time-zone limited · 🤔 Unclear (with clues such as currency, required language, location) · 🔎 No restriction stated · 🏢 On-site / Hybrid

• Badges on the job list and on the open job
• Hover for the exact sentence the verdict is based on
• Choose your country, and hide jobs you can't take
• Understands English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese and Italian postings
• Private: everything runs in your browser. No account, no server, no tracking
• Light on LinkedIn: only analyzes jobs you scroll past, throttled and cached

Not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn. Badges are a best-effort reading of the posting text and can be wrong: always check the listing.

## Single purpose
Show, on LinkedIn job listings, whether a remote job accepts candidates from the user's country.

## Permission justifications
- **storage:** saves the user's settings and caches job verdicts for 14 days to avoid repeat requests.
- **Host access (https://www.linkedin.com/*, content script):** needed to read job cards and descriptions and add badges on LinkedIn job pages. No other site is accessed.
- **Remote code:** none.
